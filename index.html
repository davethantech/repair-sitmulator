ABOUT THIS TRAINING PROTOTYPE

Real interaction. Controlled educational models.

This application contains procedural 3D laptop repair, advanced 2D boardview, 36 laptop and 11 desktop training cases, computed meter readings, virtual reassembly, digital oscilloscope, thermal visualization, USB-C analysis, isolated bench supply, mock SPI tool, advanced rework exercises, electronics MNA lab, practice examinations and work-order records. An optional Node classroom server supports user accounts and assignments.

Scope: The fictional FL-01 trainer is not a reverse-engineered ASUS motherboard. The motherboard rail model uses deterministic power dependencies and fault behavior, not full transistor-level SPICE. A separate linear MNA solver powers the low-voltage electronics lesson. The 3D model is procedural and stylized, not a licensed OEM CAD model. Oscilloscope timing, thermal camera and rework mechanics are teaching approximations, not calibrated instrumentation or manufacturing specifications.

Safety: Do not use fictional trainer readings, rework temperatures, or PSU values to repair real hardware. Follow the exact board schematic, manufacturer documentation, battery procedures and proper technician training.
