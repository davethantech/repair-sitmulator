Engineering boundariesDETERMINISTIC • DOCUMENTED • EDUCATIONAL

BOARD//LAB digital twin v4 models one intentionally small 5 V LED design with an explicit network, selected faults, load behavior, live DMM readings, derived scope traces, PCB geometry checks and repair state. It deliberately does not claim OEM laptop accuracy, calibrated thermal physics, ngspice simulation, native KiCad schematic validation, manufacturing release, or certification. Use the original 3D workbench, advanced lab and PCB Studio for other topics.
